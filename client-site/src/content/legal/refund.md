## 1. What you buy

A **pass** is prepaid, time-limited, digital access delivered to your account **immediately** after
successful payment. Value is consumed as you use it.

## 2. General rule

Because access is digital and begins immediately, purchases are **generally non-refundable** once a
pass has been activated or used.

## 3. When we refund

We refund or credit in good faith when:

- **You were charged but no working pass was delivered** due to a failure on our side.
- **Duplicate or accidental charge** — full refund of the erroneous amount.
- **Unused pass, reported within 3 days** — full refund if you have not activated or used any part
  of the pass.
- **Material service failure on our side** — fair refund or credit for the unused portion.
- **Failed/declined transaction** that still debited you — usually auto-reversed by Razorpay/your
  bank within 5–7 business days; contact us if not.

## 4. When we do not refund

- Change of mind after use.
- Unused time or budget after expiry.
- Dissatisfaction with AI output where the service worked as described.
- Accounts suspended for violating our [Terms] or [Acceptable Use Policy].

Nothing here limits non-excludable rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

## 5. How to request

Email **{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}** from your registered account email with the payment reference and a short
description. We acknowledge within **24 hours** and decide within **5–7 business days**.

Approved refunds return to your original payment method via Razorpay (typically 5–10 business days for
your bank to reflect).

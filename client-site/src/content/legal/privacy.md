## 1. What we collect

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Account** — email, name (via Clerk sign-in) | Identity, support, billing linkage |
| **Payment metadata** — amount, plan, status, Razorpay reference | Billing, fraud prevention |
| **Usage metadata** — token counts, costs, model alias, timestamps, surface (CLI/Workbench) | Metering, caps, analytics |
| **Consent records** — policy version, time, user agent | Legal compliance |
| **Support emails** you send us | Help, grievances |

**We do not collect or store:** advertising identifiers, behavioural tracking profiles, or your full
payment credentials (Razorpay handles those).

## 2. What we deliberately do not store (gateway)

Our API gateway **does not persist your prompts, code, files, or model replies**. Metering records
hold counts and costs only — not content. This is by design.

## 3. Workbench temporary storage

When you use **Workbench**, your artifact brief and run progress may be stored **temporarily** (up
to 30 days) so we can complete and resume your request. This is operational storage, not used for
training or resale. You may request erasure (Section 8).

## 4. Security signals (transient only)

We may use your IP address **in memory only** (short-lived rate limits at the edge) to prevent
abuse. We **do not store** your IP address in our databases.

## 5. Third-party processors

| Processor | Role | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Clerk** | Authentication | Email, name, auth metadata |
| **Razorpay** | Payments (India) | Payment amount, plan, reference |
| **Xiaomi MiMo** | AI inference | The prompt/content you submit for processing |
| **Cloudflare** | CDN, security | Request metadata |
| **Hostinger** | Hosting (India region) | Data processed on our infrastructure |

**Cross-border transfer:** Your inputs are sent to **Xiaomi MiMo**, whose systems may be operated
outside India. We disclose this before you consent. We strip client IP headers before forwarding to
the AI provider. Do not submit confidential or third-party personal data you would not want processed
abroad.

We **do not sell** personal data and **do not use your inputs to train our own models**.

Cross-border transfers are made as permitted under applicable Indian law. If the Government of India
restricts transfers to a jurisdiction we use, we will adjust processing accordingly.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

- **Clerk session cookie** — keeps you signed in (essential).
- **Browser local storage** — session key and UI preferences on your device (essential for the
  product to work).

We do **not** use third-party advertising or analytics trackers. See our cookie notice on first visit.
